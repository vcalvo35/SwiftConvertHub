Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SwiftConvertHub ("we", "us") operates swiftconverthub.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection and use of information when you use our site.

Information We Collect

We do not require registration or personal information to use our converters. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (pages visited, session duration, browser type) to improve our service. We also use Google AdSense to display advertisements, which may use cookies.

Cookies

We use a single localStorage key ("sch-theme") to remember your light/dark theme preference. Google Analytics and Google AdSense may also set cookies on your device.

Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to SwiftConvertHub and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics and Google AdSense are third-party services with their own privacy policies. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google AdSense by visiting Google Ads Settings. For third-party vendors generally, you can review options at aboutads.info. You can also block cookies entirely through your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

Your Data Rights

Because SwiftConvertHub doesn't require an account and doesn't store personal information on our own servers, there's no personal profile of you for us to delete directly. Any data collected happens through Google Analytics and Google AdSense under their own retention rules — you can request deletion of your Google-associated data at Google's Data & Privacy dashboard. If you have any other question about data we may hold, contact us and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

SwiftConvertHub is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our contact form, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If we change the services we use (for example, adding a new analytics or advertising provider), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. We recommend checking this page from time to time.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
